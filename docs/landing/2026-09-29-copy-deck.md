## 1. Message brief (Audience → Message → Media → Messenger)

- **Primary audience:** the owner or director general of a PyME with an ERP (the archetype "El Dueño Operativamente Ciego").
  - Wants: "margen por producto en tiempo real".
  - Today: «No saco mis datos del ERP».
- **Secondary audience:** the IT or systems lead who can block the deal.
- **Tertiary audience:** US prospects and partners, who read the English version.
- **Why they should care** (VOS pains, strongest first):
  - Every report depends on one person and takes days.
  - Data is trapped in the ERP.
  - Each area brings its own number.
  - Decisions run on last month's data or on intuition.
  - They fear that "real BI" is a big, slow, expensive consultancy.
- **What we offer:**
  - Ask a business question in Spanish and get the number, a chart and a table in seconds, on top of the systems you already use.
  - SuperDev implements it with governance: one number per KPI, read-only access, your team trained.
  - The agent is the visible proof. The offer is the governed Business Intelligence implementation, which starts with a free 30-minute call.
- **Calls to action:**
  - Primary: «Agenda tu llamada exploratoria», with "30 min · gratis · sin compromiso".
  - Secondary: «Ver demo en vivo» (in-page), then «Haz la evaluación preliminar» further down.
  - Access to the live agent: «Pide acceso al agente en vivo».
- **Sticky line:** "Tu próximo reporte, en una pregunta."
- **Media:** a link sent by hand after a conversation (WhatsApp, email), the superdev.mx case page «Ver demo en vivo», and /business-intelligence/.
- **Messenger:** the SuperDev partner who sends the invitation.
- **Tracking tag:** live end to end since 29 Sep, 05:13Z (Odoo source `agentic_bi`, id 72; superdev.mx `ec58362`). Call links use `https://superdev.mx/calendar/?utm_source=agentic_bi&utm_medium=landing`. The CRM reads only `utm_source`; the CRM medium is the existing "organic" (Alex's ruling), and `utm_medium=landing` only labels GA4. Assessment links: `https://superdev.mx/ai-readiness/?utm_source=agentic_bi&utm_medium=landing`.

**EBELI thesis (internal, not page copy):** Agentic BI (a question-to-answer agent implemented by SuperDev on the client's own ERP) for PyME owners whose reports depend on one person turns a report that takes days into an answer in seconds. The agent was live in under 2 months at the Agente de Datos client (42 users). A seconds-level claim needs our own measurement before it becomes a number on the page; until then the copy says "en segundos" only about the demo, which visitors can see for themselves.

## 2. Voice and rules checklist (applies to every line below)

- **Voice:** "tú", peer to peer, no hype. No em dashes and no exclamation marks in visible copy. "IA" only in headlines; the body says what it does. No ETL, pipeline or insights.
- **Terms:**
  - Service: «Business Intelligence». Product: «Agentic BI».
  - The case is «Agente de Datos»; never the client's name.
  - "Diagnóstico" only as «Diagnóstico IA».
  - The first project phase is "Descubrimiento".
- **Numbers, only from the catalog:**
  - 42 usuarios con acceso en lenguaje natural.
  - En vivo en menos de 2 meses (the agent).
  - 16 semanas y 12 entregables (the full project). Always say which is which.
  - ERP: Dynamics 365.
  - Gartner 30% only as "(Gartner)". Not used here.
- **No prices.** BI "se cotiza en una propuesta formal, después del Diagnóstico IA".
- **Demo data is fictitious.** Say so wherever the data shows.
- **Privacy:** link https://superdev.mx/privacidad/ from the footer and from any form.
- **Diagnóstico IA scope:** "un reporte con tu punto de partida y un plan priorizado", with no duration or deliverable count.
