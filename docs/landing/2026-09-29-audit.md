# Agentic BI demo audit: UI, UX, copy, and brand fit

- **Page:** https://agentic-bi.superdev.mx (production `c8a0a24`, Spanish view, locked state).
- **Date:** 2026-09-29.
- **Purpose:** decide what the page must become to showcase SuperDev to prospects. This audit feeds the copy deck and the landing draft.
- **Method:** design critique, UX copy review, and brand review against `context/site-messaging.md` and `.agents/product-marketing.md` (superdev.mx repo), plus a value-proposition fit check against the VentureOS VPC for "El Dueño Operativamente Ciego".
- **Sources:**
  - SITE's guidance on 28 Sep, including Alex's rulings on the tracking tag, the `/demo` path and the access form.
  - The sibling demo pages: clinic, REI, weddings, and Te Invitamos.
- **Evidence:** `img/desktop-locked-es.png`, `img/desktop-examples-es.png` and `img/mobile-es.jpg`, plus DOM inspection at 1280 × 800 and 375 × 812.

## Overall impression

The page is a well-built demo that says almost nothing to a buyer. In two seconds a PyME owner sees:
- a generic headline;
- an empty dashed box;
- a password field.

Three things about the offer never reach them:
- It solves "reports take days and depend on one person".
- It works on their ERP.
- SuperDev implements it.

The strongest assets exist but are buried:
- The four example charts are collapsed under "Análisis de ejemplo".
- The real agent is locked behind a password with no way to ask for one.

The biggest opportunity is to lead with the buyer's pain, show a real answer immediately (MXN, Spanish, chart and table), and give every visitor one clear next step.

|---|---|---|

## 3. Copy (UX copy + brand review)

The current Spanish copy is clean and correct but written for someone who already bought: it describes the tool, not the problem.

| Current copy | Issue | Direction |
|---|---|---|
| Eyebrow «Inteligencia para decidir» | Not the service's name. The site sells «Business Intelligence». | «Agentic BI · parte de nuestro servicio de Business Intelligence», or a pain eyebrow. |
| H1 «Convierte preguntas de negocio en análisis claros» | Generic, tool-first. It doesn't name who it's for or the pain. | Lead with the pain SITE already approved: «Tienes los datos, pero cada reporte tarda días y depende de una persona.» Or a short hook in the style of «¿Pedir un reporte en tu empresa tarda días?». |
| Lede «Explora tus datos con un agente que consulta, compara, visualiza y explica lo que encuentra.» | A four-verb feature list; "agente" without context. | Use the approved line: «Preguntas de negocio en palabras, contestadas con gráficas que se arman solas desde las fuentes de datos conectadas.» |
| «El agente lee un esquema compacto mediante una conexión de solo lectura.» / «Ejecuta consultas acotadas…» / «Cada resultado se valida…» | Jargon ("esquema compacto", "consultas acotadas"); breaks the voice rule "results over jargon". | Owner layer: "Conectamos tus fuentes una vez. Preguntas en español. Decides con una gráfica y una tabla." IT layer: read-only, validated read queries, limits, data doesn't move. |
| «Pregunta sobre Northwind» | Meaningless to the buyer. | «Pregúntale a una distribuidora de ejemplo», with a note that the data is fictitious. |
| «Contraseña de la demo» / «Escribe la contraseña para activar el agente de análisis.» | Assumes the visitor has a password; no path forward. | Replace with the invitation flow and «Pide acceso al agente en vivo». |
| Meta description and OG/Twitter tags | English only ("Turn business questions into clear, decision-ready analysis…"), so Spanish shares on WhatsApp preview in English. | Localize meta, OG and Twitter per language. |

What works and should stay:
- "tú".
- No hype and no exclamation marks.
- Correct Mexican Spanish.
- The FIX de Banxico footnote, which is honest and specific.
- Spanish report headers ("Ingresos (MXN)").

Rules to enforce (from `site-messaging.md` and SITE):
- No em dashes or exclamation marks in visible copy.
- "IA" only in headlines.
- No ETL, pipeline or insights.
- No prices; BI "se cotiza tras el Diagnóstico IA".
- Only catalog numbers: 42 usuarios; en vivo en < 2 meses (agent); 16 semanas con 12 entregables (project); Gartner 30% only as "(Gartner)".
- Never name Lucava; the case is «Agente de Datos».
- "Diagnóstico" only as «Diagnóstico IA».
- CTA wording is fixed; never "Agenda una llamada", "Hablemos" or "Contáctanos".

| Customer profile item (VOS, all "assumption" tags) | Addressed on the page today? | Landing section that should address it |
|---|---|---|
| Pain: data trapped in the ERP («No saco mis datos del ERP») | No | Hero + "¿Te suena?" |
| Pain: slow, manual reporting; depends on one person | No | Hero (approved line) + before/after |
| Pain: decisions by intuition, no single source of truth | No | "¿Te suena?" + "Un solo número" |
| Pain: fear that real BI means a big, slow, expensive consultancy | No | "Cómo trabajamos" (diagnóstico → implementación, en vivo en < 2 meses) + FAQ |

The page reaches problem–solution fit only for visitors who already understand the offer. Every severe pain in the profile is currently unaddressed.

## 5. Trust, data and IT layer (BI and data-governance lens)

The demo already has strong, credible safeguards, but it states them in engineering terms or not at all. For the IT gatekeeper, the buyer who can block the deal, the landing should say, in plain language:
- **Access is read-only.** The agent can't change or delete anything, and only validated read queries run.
- **The data stays where it is.** "Sin mudar tus datos": we connect on top of Dynamics 365, Odoo, SAP B1 or Airtable.
- **Who sees what.** Permissions by role, and one agreed definition per number ("un solo número").
- **Limits.** There are query timeouts and row limits. When a question is ambiguous, the agent asks or states its assumption. It never invents numbers.
- **Privacy.**
  - The demo uses fictitious data.
  - In a real project, personal data follows the privacy notice.
  - Questions are processed by a model provider through SuperDev's gateway. Whether the gateway logs requests is not yet verified, and it must be confirmed before any claim is written.
- **Governance as a deliverable:**
  - A glossary entry and one owner for every KPI.
  - A named owner per source.
  - Quality thresholds on the fields key metrics depend on.
  This is the "BI-specific governance minimum" and the honest counter to the fear that BI is a big, slow consultancy.

The BI maturity ladder already on superdev.mx (descriptivo → diagnóstico → predictivo → prescriptivo) can frame the IT section. It says honestly that this agent covers the first two rungs well today.

## 6. Accessibility

- **Contrast:** 12 px text at 55 % opacity (the exchange-rate footnote) and 12 px coloured eyebrows sit near or below 4.5:1 on `#F7F8FA`. Raise footnote contrast, or use at least 14 px on desktop and 16 px on mobile per the guidelines.
- **Meta language:** English meta on Spanish pages hurts screen-reader and share contexts. Localize it.
- **Keep:** visible focus rings, labelled inputs, `aria-pressed` on the ES/EN switch, the live-region notices, and the ≥ 44 px targets on the main buttons.

## What works well

- The live demo is genuinely impressive:
  - It answers in Spanish with exact MXN figures (Bebidas $4,779,116.56 MXN).
  - It builds a full report with a Spanish title, chart and table ("Cliente" / "Ingresos (MXN)").
  - It orders bars by value.
  - It answers English questions in English on the Spanish page.
- Clean, calm UI: Geist, one blue accent, good spacing, light and dark themes, a tidy ES/EN switch.
- The language switch keeps the demo unlocked, restarts the conversation and remembers the choice.
- The exchange-rate footnote is honest and specific.

## Priority recommendations (feed the copy deck and landing draft)

1. **Lead with the buyer's pain and SuperDev's offer.** Pain-first hero with the approved lines, the canonical CTAs, "by SuperDev", and ties to the Business Intelligence service. This is the difference between a demo and a sales page.
2. **Show a real answer to everyone.** A public scripted replay of a Spanish report replaces the empty box and the password dead end. The live agent is unlocked by invitation, and «Pide acceso» goes to the new request form.
3. **Speak to the IT gatekeeper in plain language.** Read-only access, data stays put, one number per KPI, limits, privacy, and governance as a deliverable.
4. **Use proof only from the catalog.** Agente de Datos: 42 users, live in under 2 months, the 16-week governed project.
5. **Explain how it works and how SuperDev works.** Three plain steps, then llamada exploratoria → Diagnóstico IA → implementación, with no prices, and an FAQ about limits.
6. **Fix the small things.** Localized meta and OG tags, footnote contrast, the scatter legend, the intermittent month and language mismatch, and `/demo` for the live demo.
